About Me

Hello, I'm a designer who loves to create

With a decade of experience in UI/UX design, I specialize in creating intuitive, user-centered digital experiences that not only look beautiful but solve real problems. My approach combines strategic thinking with creative execution.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me in the kitchen experimenting with new recipes, tending to my garden, or crafting handmade creations. I believe that creativity flows across all mediums, and these passions inform my design work in unexpected ways.

I'm passionate about accessibility, design systems, and creating products that make people's lives better and more joyful.

What I Do Best

User Research

Conducting interviews, usability testing, and data analysis to uncover user needs and validate design decisions.

Interface Design

Creating beautiful, accessible interfaces that balance aesthetics with functionality and usability.

Prototyping

Building interactive prototypes that bring ideas to life and facilitate stakeholder collaboration.

Design Systems

Developing scalable component libraries and guidelines that ensure consistency across products.
